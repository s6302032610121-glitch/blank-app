## 2025-01-25 - [Enhancing Blank Templates]
**Learning:** For blank or minimal app templates, small UX touches like page configuration, toast notifications, and structured sidebars significantly improve the "first-run" experience and provide better guidance for new developers. Using descriptive link text instead of raw URLs is a simple but effective accessibility win.
**Action:** Always include `st.set_page_config`, a welcoming toast, and a clear sidebar for auxiliary information in Streamlit templates. Ensure all links have descriptive labels.
