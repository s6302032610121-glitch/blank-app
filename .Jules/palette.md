## 2025-05-14 - Welcoming Onboarding for Blank Streamlit Apps
**Learning:** For minimal app templates, a combination of a one-time welcome toast and a "Getting Started" sidebar significantly reduces the cognitive load for new users and provides immediate, actionable next steps.
**Action:** Always include a session-state-guarded welcome toast and a sidebar with descriptive documentation links when initializing a new Streamlit project.
