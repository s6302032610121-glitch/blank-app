## 2026-01-22 - [Enhancing Blank Templates]
**Learning:** For a "Blank App" template, micro-UX improvements should focus on reducing the "empty state" friction. A sidebar with immediate setup instructions and a welcoming "toast" notification significantly improves the first-run experience.
**Action:** Always check if a blank or minimal app can benefit from a "Getting Started" sidebar and descriptive link text for better accessibility.
